# Customer Service — Calculated Findings

24 synthetic support tickets, January–April 2025. CSAT scale: 1–5.

- Tickets: **24**
- Average first response: **15.96 minutes**
- Average resolution: **6.55 hours**
- Average CSAT: **3.79 / 5**
- Open tickets: **2**, including **1 high-priority** ticket

## Findings
- Chat: fastest response (**4.25 minutes**) and highest channel CSAT (**4.50/5**).
- Email: slowest response (**32 minutes**) and CSAT **3.44/5**.
- Refund issues have lowest issue-level CSAT (**2.50/5**).
- David and Grace have highest agent-level CSAT (**4.00/5** each). John has slowest average response (**22.83 minutes**) and resolution (**9.82 hours**).

## Recommendations
1. Review email queue staffing, routing and response templates.
2. Map the refund journey for policy, handoff and communication issues.
3. Audit open high-priority tickets daily.
4. Coach using case complexity and quality reviews, not raw speed alone.

**Limitations:** Small synthetic sample; no SLA targets, reopen counts, customer IDs or case complexity. Agent comparisons are not formal performance ratings.
